# THE-PLATFORM

> **Working repository name only.** “THE-PLATFORM” is a temporary project label, not a final product or brand name. Naming, trademark screening, domain availability, and handle availability are intentionally deferred until the product direction is mature enough to support a proper naming exercise.

A separate, integrated digital ecosystem intended to let people socialise, create, consume media, run businesses, discover and purchase products and services, organise work, attend events, and manage transactions without repeatedly leaving the platform.

This repository preserves the product vision, feature inventory, principles, and decisions as they are developed. It is a product-definition repository at this stage, not an implementation specification or commitment to build every capability at launch.

## Product identity and boundaries

- This is an independent product and venture.
- **ShadeFast is a separate product** with its own identity, purpose, architecture, users, and business model. This platform does not replace, absorb, extend, or redefine ShadeFast.
- **HDIP (Hybrid Decentralised Identity Passport)** is a separate identity infrastructure project that may provide identity and credential verification capabilities to this platform. Integration does not make HDIP a product feature owned exclusively by this platform.
- The platform's final public name has not been selected. Do not treat this repository name as a naming decision.

## Product thesis

**One platform. Multiple dedicated experiences. No unnecessary exits.**

The platform should feel like one coherent product while giving each activity an interface and interaction model designed for its purpose. Text conversations should not be forced into a video feed; shopping should not behave like entertainment; meetings should not feel like social scrolling.

The intended experience spans:

- Social networking and communication
- Video, music, podcasts, audiobooks, and live streaming
- Verified product commerce and managed marketplace transactions
- Restaurants, food ordering, delivery, and on-demand services
- Transportation and appointment booking
- Meetings, collaboration, events, movie parties, and conferences
- Payments, creator earnings, merchant settlements, and other financial capabilities
- Shared trust, safety, identity, privacy, search, and AI infrastructure

## Commercial thesis

The business model is not limited to advertising and premium subscriptions. Potential revenue sources include marketplace commissions, transaction and service fees, delivery and fulfilment economics, creator and event tools, advertising, subscriptions, and other value-added services.

The platform intends to support monetisation eligibility from the point a user completes the required HDIP-backed verification and satisfies the applicable product, policy, legal, and safety requirements. Eligibility is not a promise of earnings or a guarantee that every monetisation method is available to every account.

## Working rules

1. Preserve ideas before pruning scope.
2. Distinguish confirmed direction from proposals and unresolved questions.
3. Do not silently turn a discussion point into a binding product decision.
4. Do not conflate this platform with ShadeFast or HDIP.
5. Do not select a final name, domain, or brand identity by assumption.
6. Expand features into specifications before designing implementation architecture.
7. Treat trust, privacy, security, compliance, and operational accountability as product requirements, not later add-ons.
