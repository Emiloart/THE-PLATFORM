# Axiely

An integrated digital ecosystem intended to let people socialise, create and consume media, run businesses, discover and purchase products and services, organise work, attend events, and manage transactions within one connected environment.

This repository preserves the product vision, feature inventory, principles, and decisions as they develop. It is a product-definition repository, not an implementation specification or a commitment to deliver every capability at launch.

## Product thesis

**One platform. Multiple dedicated experiences. No unnecessary exits.**

The platform should feel like one coherent product while giving each activity an interface and interaction model designed for its purpose. Text conversations should not be forced into a video feed; shopping should not behave like entertainment; meetings should not feel like social scrolling.

The intended experience spans:

- Social networking and communication
- Video, music, podcasts, audiobooks, and live streaming
- Verified product commerce and managed marketplace transactions
- Restaurants, food ordering, delivery, and on-demand services
- Transportation and appointment booking
- Meetings, collaboration, events, movie parties, and conferences
- Payments, creator earnings, merchant settlements, and other financial capabilities
- Shared trust, safety, identity, privacy, search, and AI infrastructure

## Identity and verification

HDIP (Hybrid Decentralised Identity Passport) is intended to provide an identity and credential verification foundation for verified participation. The integration model, credential requirements, and role-specific checks will be specified separately.

## Current status

**Discovery and product definition.** The feature catalogue is an initial inventory derived from the product concept and founder discussions. It is intentionally broad. Features are not yet prioritised, fully specified, technically validated, or approved for a particular release.

## Working rules

1. Preserve ideas before pruning scope.
2. Distinguish confirmed direction from proposals and unresolved questions.
3. Do not silently turn a discussion point into a binding product decision.
4. Use Axiely consistently in product documentation and user-facing materials.
5. Expand features into specifications before approving implementation architecture.
6. Treat trust, privacy, security, compliance, and operational accountability as product requirements, not later add-ons.
